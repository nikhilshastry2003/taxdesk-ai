## Phase 0: Discovery

### Concept: Requirements Gathering / Workflow Mapping

**What it is:** Understanding how a user actually works *before* writing code. Watching them, taking notes, identifying pain points. The goal is not to confirm what you already think — it's to discover what you don't know.

**Why it matters:** Every assumption you make about dad's workflow that's wrong = code you have to rewrite later. An hour of observation saves days of rebuilding.

**Key takeaway:** Build for what dad *actually* does, not what we *think* he does.

**Questions:**
- Q: Why not just start coding and iterate?
- A: Iterating on assumptions is expensive. Iterating on real user feedback is valuable. The first iteration should be against reality, not guesses.
