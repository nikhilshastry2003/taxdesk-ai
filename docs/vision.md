# TaxDesk Vision

## What TaxDesk Is

TaxDesk is a local-first compliance command center for small Indian tax practitioners.

The first design partner is Nikhil's dad. The first job is simple: replace his Excel tick-mark checklist for pending compliance work.

TaxDesk should help him answer this every morning:

```text
Which clients have pending GSTR-3B, GSTR-1, EPF, or ESI work, and where is the related proof/document?
```

## Who It Is For

TaxDesk is for:

- Solo tax practitioners.
- GST consultants.
- Small accounting offices.
- Bookkeepers who manage recurring compliance work for many clients.

The first version is for one office and one primary user. It should fit dad's real workflow before trying to support every consultant in India.

## Who It Is Not For Yet

TaxDesk is not currently for:

- Large CA firms.
- Enterprise accounting teams.
- Multi-branch offices.
- SaaS customers with separate user roles.
- Companies wanting a Tally replacement.

## Product Boundaries

TaxDesk is not:

- A Tally replacement.
- A GST portal automation tool.
- An AI-first chatbot.
- A RAG app in version 1.
- A multi-agent system.
- A SaaS product yet.

## MVP Promise

The MVP wins if dad opens TaxDesk before opening Excel for monthly compliance tracking.

If TaxDesk does not save time or reduce missed work, AI features do not matter.

## Later Vision

After the tracker is useful, TaxDesk can become smarter:

- Index local client folders.
- Search saved returns, challans, acknowledgements, and salary sheets.
- Add document retrieval.
- Add RAG over selected documents with citations.
- Add an assistant that answers from structured data and local documents.

AI should enter only after the base product becomes useful.
